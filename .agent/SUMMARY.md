## Becoming a Manager: Your Path to Leadership

Managers (formerly called Recruiters) unlock the highest earning potential by building and leading a network of agents.

### Step 1 — Build Your Team
You'll receive a special referral link to invite new agents. Anyone who joins using your link becomes part of your Manager Network. Share this link everywhere—social media, email, in-person meetings, and business events.

### Step 2 — Hit the Targets (Within 90 Days)
To unlock Manager status, you must achieve specific performance goals within 90 days:
- **30 active affiliates (agents)** in your network
- **100 total businesses closed** across your entire network

*"Active" means the agent has signed at least one paying business, not just registered.*

### Step 3 — Unlock Massive Rewards
Once you hit the targets, you unlock:
- Ongoing earnings as long as businesses are paying (no fixed time limit)
- **5% team commission** on all businesses your network brings in
- Cash bonuses or incentives from performance campaigns (optional)

> **Important for Top Managers:** Don't just recruit—**train your agents**. Share your pitch, help them close deals, track their performance, and focus on building active agents who consistently close deals. The stronger your team, the more you earn. Quality matters more than quantity.

## FAQs & Getting Started

**Q: What if a business stops paying?**
Your commission pauses during inactive months but resumes when they start paying again. You don't lose the business permanently—it remains linked to you.

**Q: When do I get paid?**
Payments are made monthly and tracked through your dashboard. All earnings are visible in real-time.

**Q: Do I need experience?**
No experience required. You'll receive simple pitch scripts, demo tools (QR codes), and guidance on closing businesses.

**Q: Is there an earnings limit?**
No limit. Your income depends on the number of businesses you bring in and agents in your network.

**Q: Can I be both Agent and Manager?**
Yes! You earn from personal sales (15%) plus team sales (5%)—two income streams simultaneously.

**Q: What businesses should I target?**
Restaurants, fashion stores, salons, barbers, supermarkets, offices, and service providers—any business with customers.
